In our model, additional covariates may be included to account for periodic
noise present in the signal, such as heart-rate and repiration. We model these
physiological fluctuations from the data as a preprocessing step. We use an
auto-regressive process of order 2. The resaon we choose an AR model over ARMA
model is that it allows us to use method of moments rather than maximum
likelihood procedures to estimate the noise parameters. This speeds-up
compuration time when repeatedly fitting the model to tens of thousands of time
series.
