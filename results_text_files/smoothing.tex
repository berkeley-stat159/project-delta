Fall 2015 group delta project
=======
Prior to statistical analysis, we perform a series of preprocessing steps to 
minimize the influence of data acquisition and physiological artifacts.This  
will help us validate statistical assumptions and standardize the locations of
brain regions across subjects in group analysis. We start by convolving the 
functional images with a Gaussian kernel (described by the full width of the 
kernel at half its maximum height). Data were spatially smoothed using a 5 mm 
FWHM Gaussian kernel.This is equivalent to applying a low-pass filter prior to
construction thus the acquired data is discarded as a byproduct of smoothing and
temporal resolution is sacraficed. We apply a scipy package called scipy.ndimage
to achieve this goal.
